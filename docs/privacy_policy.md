# Privacy Policy — CivilCal

**Last updated: 21 October 2026**

CivilCal is published by Bisaas. This policy explains what the CivilCal
mobile app (Android and iOS) does with your information, why it does it,
and what you can do about it.

The web version of the same product is covered by the same policy; where
the web app collects something the mobile app does not, this document
says so explicitly.

---

## 1. The short version

- We collect what the app needs to work, and we tell you exactly what
  that is.
- We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** use it
  for advertising. There is no advertising SDK in the app.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.
- You can change your privacy choices at any time in
  **Settings → Privacy**, and you can erase your anonymous profile from
  the same place.
- You can ask us to export or delete your account at any time by writing
  to **privacy@bisaas.com**.

---

## 2. Who we are

Bisaas ("we", "us") operates CivilCal, a study and practice application
for engineering students. Our website is <https://bisaas.com>. For
anything about this policy, write to **privacy@bisaas.com**.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, this policy is also our

## 3. What we collect, and why

### 3.1 When you create an account

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address | To sign you in, to send the daily-quiz reminder, and to recover your account |
| Password | To sign you in. We store only a one-way hash of it. The app keeps your password in the device keystore for the few seconds it takes to complete the sign-in, and never writes it anywhere else |
| Display name | To show next to your scores and your leaderboard position |
| Profile picture | Only if you choose one. You pick a single image from your photo library; the app never reads the rest of your library and never opens the camera |
| Preferred language | To show the app in your language |
| Referral code | Only if you enter one, to credit a referral reward |

### 3.2 While you use the app

Everything you do in the app is sent to our servers, because your
progress has to be there when you sign in on another device:

- quiz attempts, your answers, your scores and how long you took
- your streak, XP, coins, achievements and leaderboard position
- battle records and your answer timings
- your calculator history and the calculations you saved
- the courses you started, the notes and bookmarks you made, and the
  offline packs you downloaded
- your AI tutor conversations and the calculator-assist prompts you sent
- the questions you asked the AI assistant

This is **app functionality**. It cannot be switched off without
breaking the product, and it is deleted when you delete your account.

### 3.3 Device identifiers and diagnostics

| What | Exactly what it is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Install id | A random identifier (UUID v4) generated by the app on your device and kept in the device's secure keystore. It is not derived from your hardware, so it cannot be used to recognise your device across apps, and it is destroyed when you uninstall the app. | Ties a session to an install so that a token stolen from one device is not accepted from another |
| Push notification token | The identifier Firebase Cloud Messaging uses to deliver notifications to this install. We delete it when you sign out. | To send you the daily-quiz reminder, achievement unlocks and battle invitations |
| Device name | A readable name for this install, such as "Redmi 6A" | Shown on your own "active sessions" list so you can tell your devices apart |
| App version and platform | e.g. `1.0.2`, `android` | To serve you a compatible build, and to enforce a minimum version when a release changes the API |
| Device integrity signal | A single coarse label — `clean`, `suspicious` or `compromised` — derived on your device by a security library. It says only *this install shows a signal such as root or an attached debugger*. It is **not** an identifier, and the individual signals never leave your device. | To detect and respond to a tampered or repackaged copy of the app |
| Crash reports | The error, the stack trace, your app version, device model, OS version, language and network type. | To find and fix crashes |
| Usage events (optional) | The name of an event — for example `quiz_start` — and a small set of parameters. **No question content is ever included.** These are tied to a device identifier, not to your account. | To find out which features are actually useful, and which are not |

The crash reports and the usage events are handled by Google Firebase.
See [section 7](#7-who-processes-your-information).

### 3.4 Before you create an account

If you use the app without signing in, we still hold a small anonymous
profile so the app can work and so we can recommend calculators:

- an anonymous identifier that our server issues and signs — your device
  never sends an identifier it could have made up itself
- which calculators you looked at
- your consent choice

You can see and erase this profile from **Settings → Privacy → Data
stored about this device**. Once the anonymous profile is linked to a
real account, erasing it is handled as an account deletion instead.

### 3.5 Support

If you email us, we keep your message and your email address so we can
answer you. Support messages are not used for marketing or analytics.

## 4. What we do **not** do

- We do **not** sell your personal information.
- We do **not** use your information for advertising, and there is no
  advertising SDK in the app.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.
- We do **not** collect your contacts, messages, call log, photos
  library, microphone or location.
- We do **not** put your question answers into analytics events.
- We do **not** back up the app's data to the cloud. The app's local
  data — including your sign-in token and your cached answers — never
  leaves the device, not even through a phone-to-phone transfer.

---

## 5. Your choices

**Analytics.** The app asks on first launch. You can change the answer
at any time in **Settings → Privacy**. Turning analytics off stops
usage events from that moment on; it does not delete events that were
already collected.

**Notifications.** You can turn push notifications off in your device
settings at any time. We still send the request for a push token, but
no notification reaches you.

**Device integrity.** The integrity check runs on your device so that a
tampered copy of the app can be refused a session. It reports a coarse
band and nothing more, and it is not a way of identifying you.

**Erasure.** *Settings → Privacy → Data stored about this device* shows
what we hold about the anonymous profile and erases it. For your
account, write to **privacy@bisaas.com** and we will delete the account
and its data.

## 6. How long we keep things

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| The install id, the cached quiz answers and the sign-in token | On your device only, until you sign out, uninstall, or the token expires |
| The push token | Until you sign out, or until you uninstall |
| Your account and your learning activity | Until you delete your account, then removed from our active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days |
| Crash reports | Up to 90 days |
| Usage events | Up to 14 months in aggregate form, then only counts are kept |
| Anonymous visitor profile and intent events | Until you erase it, or 13 months after your last visit |
| Server request logs | 30 days |

Idempotency keys — the temporary records that stop a repeated request
from being counted twice — are pruned after 7 days.

## 7. Who processes your information

| Processor | What it handles |
|---|---|
| Google Firebase (Crashlytics) | Crash reports and stack traces |
| Google Firebase (Analytics) | Usage events, only if you opted in |
| Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging) | Push delivery, and your push token |
| Google Firebase (Realtime Database) | Realtime features of the app |
| Sentry | Crash reporting, only if we have configured it for your build |

Each of these acts as a processor on our instructions and is bound to the
data-processing terms of the platform it runs on. Firebase's services can
be pinned to a data region; the region currently in use is
`asia-southeast1`.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own
purposes, and we do not disclose it for legal purposes except where we
are legally compelled to.

## 8. Where your information is stored

Our servers are in South-East Asia, and the Firebase project is
configured for the `asia-southeast1` region. If you are in the EEA, the
UK or Switzerland, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses published
by the European Commission for transfers to a third country.

## 9. Security

- Every connection to our servers is encrypted with TLS, and release
  builds additionally pin the certificate, so a substituted certificate
  is rejected rather than trusted.
- Your sign-in token is stored in the device keystore or keychain, never
  in ordinary app storage.
- App data is excluded from Android backups and from device-to-device
  transfers.
- The app refuses to run a production build that does not have a
  certificate pin configured.

No system is perfect. If you find a vulnerability, report it to
**security@bisaas.com** and we will acknowledge it within three working
days.

the local threshold). We do not knowingly collect personal information

write to **privacy@bisaas.com** and we will delete it.
