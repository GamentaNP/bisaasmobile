What is new in 1.0.2

- The launcher icon is now the CivilCal mark, on both Android and iOS.
- Push notifications now arrive on iOS while the app is closed. Achievement unlocks, battle invitations and the daily-quiz reminder no longer wait for you to open the app.
- Choosing a profile picture no longer crashes on iOS.
- The privacy choices screen now lists exactly what the app sends, and the buttons stay reachable on a small screen.
- The app tells you where to update when a new version is required, with a link that actually works.
- Smaller download: the release no longer ships an unused processor slice.
